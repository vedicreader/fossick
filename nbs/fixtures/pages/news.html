Council approves new cycle lanes for city centre

The £12m plan adds 9 km of protected lanes by 2028, but some traders fear the loss of parking.

By Tom Lee, 6 November 2026

The city council voted 31 to 12 on Thursday to approve a network of protected cycle lanes through the city centre.

The plan, which will cost £12 million, adds 9 km of lanes separated from traffic by kerbs. Work on the first section, along King Street and Bridge Road, starts in January.

"This is the biggest change to our streets in a generation," said councillor Priya Shah, who leads on transport. "People told us they would cycle if they felt safe. Now they will."

Traders' concerns

About 140 parking spaces will be removed. The traders' association said that some shops depend on customers who drive.

The council said that a study of the King Street trial found that footfall rose by 6% after the lane opened. Read our report on the trial.
